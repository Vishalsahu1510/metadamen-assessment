# VAANI™ 5–7 Minute Demo Video Script & Storyboard

This guide provides a structured minute-by-minute walkthrough to record your submission video for MetaDamen.

---

## Video Specifications
- **Target Duration**: 5 to 7 Minutes
- **Key Flow**: Profile Setup $\rightarrow$ Interview Start $\rightarrow$ Candidate Answering (Voice/Text) $\rightarrow$ Real-time AI Evaluation $\rightarrow$ Adaptive Follow-up & Difficulty Shift $\rightarrow$ Project Claim Probing $\rightarrow$ Final Assessment Report $\rightarrow$ Dashboard Analytics & Dossier Export.

---

## Timeline & Narration Script

### Minute 0:00 – 1:00 | Introduction & Architecture Overview
- **Visual**: Show the landing page of VAANI™ with the dark-mode recruitment intelligence UI.
- **Narrate**:
  > *"Hello! This is a demonstration of VAANI™ — Adaptive AI Interview and Assessment Intelligence built for the MetaDamen internship assignment. Unlike generic chatbots, VAANI is designed to simulate a real-world technical interview that dynamically adapts to the candidate's background, evaluates answers across 6 core metrics in real time, verifies technical resume claims, and algorithmically generates a comprehensive readiness report."*
- **Visual**: Show the Candidate Profile form. Click the **"⚡ Load MetaDamen Scenario"** button to instantly populate the exact candidate scenario from the assignment:
  - 3rd-Year B.Tech CSE / AI-ML student
  - Target Role: Associate Software Engineer at MetaDamen
  - Declared Skills: Python, Machine Learning, FastAPI, React, SQL
  - Project: Recommendation System with claim *"Achieved 95% model accuracy and sub-20ms latency"*.
- **Narrate**:
  > *"We click 'Begin Adaptive Interview'. VAANI initializes the session state, builds the candidate skill profile, and crafts a personalized introductory question."*

---

### Minute 1:00 – 2:30 | Stage 1 (Introduction) & Stage 2 (Technical Assessment)
- **Visual**: Show the Live Interview Room.
  - Highlight the **VAANI™ AI Recruiter Avatar**: Watch as the animated recruiter face speaks the question automatically with real-time lip-sync, animated sound equalizer bars, and eye blinking!
  - Point out the **Azure Neural 24kHz Voice** quality: clear, human-like cadence with natural intonation.
  - Open the voice settings slider to demonstrate switching recruiter voices (*Ava, Andrew, Jenny, or Indian English Neerja*).
  - Click **"Speak Answer"** to demonstrate continuous voice transcription without 2-second timeout cutoffs.
  - Submit the answer.
- **Visual**: Point to the **Real-Time AI Evaluation Drawer** on the right:
  - Overall score (e.g. 8/10), Technical Accuracy, Communication, and Depth.
  - Highlight the **Live Skill Profile** bars automatically updating with the exponential moving average.
- **Visual**: VAANI transitions to Stage 2 (`Technical Assessment`).
  - Show how difficulty adapted (e.g. Medium $\rightarrow$ Hard or Medium $\rightarrow$ Easy based on the score).
  - The Avatar automatically speaks the new question out loud!

---

### Minute 2:30 – 4:00 | Stage 3: Project Deep Dive & Claim Verification (Section 10)
- **Visual**: Show VAANI asking the Project Deep Dive question.
- **Narrate**:
  > *"Notice how VAANI specifically detected the candidate's claim from the profile: 'Achieved 95% model accuracy'. Instead of asking a generic question, VAANI probes how data leakage was prevented, the train-test split strategy, and which evaluation metric was used."*
- **Visual**: Provide an answer explaining time-based splits and ROC-AUC.
- **Visual**: Show the AI Evaluation detecting the claim and scoring technical accuracy. Show the dynamic difficulty scaling logic in action (Section 9).

---

### Minute 4:00 – 5:30 | Stage 4 (Problem Solving) & Stage 5 (Behavioral / Final Report)
- **Visual**: Answer the Problem Solving question (e.g. debugging latency bottlenecks).
- **Visual**: Click **"Generate Final Report"** (or complete the final behavioral question).
- **Narrate**:
  > *"Now that the interview is concluded, VAANI synthesizes the full transcript, evaluations, and skill matrices into the official candidate assessment dossier."*

---

### Minute 5:30 – 7:00 | Assessment Dashboard & Hiring Decision Walkthrough
- **Visual**: Walk through the **Assessment Dashboard**:
  1. **5 Core Assessment Area Scores**:
     - Technical Knowledge %
     - Problem Solving %
     - Communication %
     - Project Understanding %
     - Behavioral %
     - Composite Overall Readiness %
  2. **Competency Radar Matrix**: Visual polygon mapping candidate strengths.
  3. **Adaptive Difficulty Timeline**: Line graph displaying question difficulty and score progression.
  4. **Algorithmic Recommendation**: Explain that the badge (*"Placement Ready"* or *"Strong Candidate"*) is computed mathematically from the metrics, not hardcoded.
  5. **Demonstrated Strengths, Gaps, and Recommended Learning courses**.
  6. **Complete Question-by-Question Transcript Accordion**.
  7. **Export Dossier**: Click *"Export JSON Dossier"* or *"Print Report"*.
  8. Click the **"Archive"** tab to show that previous interviews are saved and can be inspected anytime.
- **Closing**:
  > *"Thank you! VAANI fulfills every requirement of the MetaDamen internship assignment, running reliably with both cloud LLMs and zero-key local fallbacks."*
